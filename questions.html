Sustainability in SLC

FAQs

What items can and can't go in Salt Lake City's blue recycling bins?

  • You can recycle clean paper, flattened cardboard, metal cans, and most plastic bottles and jugs labeled #1-#7 (as long as they're rinsed). Do not include plastic bags, Styrofoam, greasy pizza boxes, or food waste, as these contaminate the load.

How should I handle items like batteries, light bulbs, and electronics?

  • These items cannot go in regular bins because they contain hazardous materials. Salt Lake City provides drop-off locations for safe disposal and recycling, including the Household Hazardous Waste Program and many local stores.

What do I do with glass? Can it go in curbside recycling?

  • Glass has its own recycling stream. Curbside glass recycling is available, or you can drop glass off at designated purple bins throughout the city. All colors are accepted, but lids and caps should be removed.

Is composting available in Salt Lake City, and how can I start?

  • You can compost at home with a backyard bin or sign up for curbside composting. Composting turns food scraps and yard waste into nutrient-rich soil instead of sending it to the landfill.

What are some simple ways to live more sustainably in the city?

  • Walk, bike, or take public transit whenever possible.
  • Bring reusable bags, bottles, and cups.
  • Support local farmers markets and thrift stores.
  • Turn off lights and unplug chargers when not in use.
  • Choose products with minimal packaging.
  • These actions might seem small, but together they make a big impact on reducing waste, saving energy, and keeping Salt Lake City clean.
